Key Operational Observations

💡 1. Authentication Queue Stagnation

Access & Security drives the extreme max durations (16.3 business days / 69 calendar days). Low-complexity Active Directory and login tickets are sitting unassigned in general queues due to a lack of automated triage rules.

💡 2. Out-of-Hours Calendar Lag

The 3.1x ratio between Average Actual Duration (3d 8h) and Average Business Duration (1d 1h) confirms that weekend and non-business delays represent the vast majority of total elapsed resolution time for P4 tickets.

💡 3. Vendor Interface Dependencies

While Integration & Interfaces maintains a fast average business duration (22 hrs 28 mins), third-party NHS system dependencies (such as WPAS / GP Surgeries) create severe long-tail delays (up to 56 calendar days).
